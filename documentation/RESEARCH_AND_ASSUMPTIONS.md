This file has three parts: the research log, the workspace inspection (environment baseline), and the assumptions and open questions.

**Rules** (from CLAUDE.md §3):

- Every entry names the source actually checked. "Empirical" means the claim was proven by a command or test executed in this workspace, and that command is quoted.
- Confidence levels: **High** means an official source plus an executed check. **Medium** means an official source, or an executed check, but not both. **Low** means indirect evidence only.
